# Community conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

- Be respectful and specific. Critique an idea or result rather than the person presenting it.
- Report experiments honestly, including regressions, uncertainty and missing evidence.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, or publish another person's private information.
- Do not submit credentials, proprietary datasets or material you lack permission to distribute.
- Respect maintainers' time by providing reproducible examples and keeping discussions relevant.

Maintainers may edit/remove disruptive content or restrict participation to protect the community. For a sensitive conduct concern, request a private contact channel without posting personal details in a public issue. Security issues should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These rules apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions. This document is original project guidance and does not claim a third-party enforcement service.
